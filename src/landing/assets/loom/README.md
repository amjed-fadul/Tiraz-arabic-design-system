# From thread to interface — landing imagery

Four original images that explain how weaving relates to building interfaces.
Each pairs a craft material with one idea from the product, so the pictures
carry meaning instead of decorating the page with generic cultural symbols.

| Asset                | Where it appears            | Idea                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `first-thread.webp`  | Why Tiraz · 01 Arabic first | One thread crosses the warp from right to left, the direction Arabic is read.        |
| `draft.webp`         | Why Tiraz · 02 One system   | A weaving draft is a design system: rules on one grid produce a coherent cloth.      |
| `pattern-cards.webp` | Why Tiraz · 03 Ready for AI | Jacquard cards were the first machine-readable instructions, like the AI guidance.   |
| `finished-band.webp` | Closing                     | A finished band whose first threads are still visible: from the first thread onward. |

The hero itself uses no image. Its woven band is drawn live in WebGL, and the
inscription is rendered from the page font, so the Arabic is never generated.

## Provenance

Generated on 2026-10-15 with Figma's image generation (`gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`)
in the Figma file
[Tiraz landing — Night loom imagery](https://www.figma.com/design/71IFnchQBPsgfOr6ZCWHxd)
(nodes 7:2, 9:2, 10:2 and 11:2). The originals are 960 × 1280 PNG (portrait) and
1536 × 864 PNG (closing). The project files were encoded with
`cwebp -q 84 -m 6`, without resizing or cropping. No text, UI, logos or effects are
embedded, and no stock or third-party images were used. The images are
distributed with this project's license.

## Prompts

**first-thread.webp** — Extreme macro studio photograph of a hand loom. Dozens of
taut ivory cotton warp threads run vertically and perfectly parallel through the
frame, razor-sharp in the middle focal plane. A single deep forest-green thread is
being passed horizontally through them, travelling from the right side of the
frame toward the left, going over and under alternate warp threads; its leading
end is still loose on the left. At the bottom of the frame a few tight rows of
finished ivory-and-green plain weave have already formed. Near-black background,
one soft raking key light from the upper right revealing fibre texture and cotton
fuzz, shallow depth of field, calm, precise, minimal contemporary craft
photography, fine film grain. No hands, no people, no text, no letters, no logos.

**draft.webp** — Overhead editorial studio photograph on a dark charcoal linen
table. On the right: a sheet of weaver's point paper, a hand-inked weaving draft
made only of a precise grid of small squares, some squares filled with deep
forest-green ink forming a symmetrical stepped geometric band motif, with small
threading and tie-up grid blocks along its edges. On the left: the small
hand-woven ivory cotton swatch produced from that draft, its woven deep-green
geometric band exactly matching the inked pattern. A fine black ink pen and a few
loose ivory and green threads lie between them. Single soft window light from the
upper right, deep shadows toward the edges, precise, calm, minimal, fine film
grain. The paper contains only grid squares: no numbers, no letters, no words, no
handwriting, no logos.

**pattern-cards.webp** — Moody macro studio photograph of a chain of antique
Jacquard loom punch cards: stiff cream-coloured cards perforated with precise
grids of small round holes, laced together edge to edge with linen cord, cascading
in a gentle curve down toward a loom, where they guide the weaving of a precise
ivory and deep forest-green geometric woven band visible at the bottom of the
frame. Warm single raking light from the upper right, near-black background,
shallow depth of field focused on the holes of the nearest card, faint dust in the
light, tactile, precise, quietly technical, fine film grain. No text, no numbers,
no letters, no logos, no people, no hands.

**finished-band.webp** — Cinematic wide studio photograph. A long narrow
hand-woven band of ivory cotton with a continuous deep forest-green and charcoal
rectilinear geometric pattern (stepped, symmetrical, precise) lies unrolled
diagonally across a dark charcoal table. It emerges from a loose roll at the far
right and ends on the left with its unwoven warp threads still loose and fanned
out like a fringe, showing where the weaving began. Single soft raking light from
the upper right, deep shadows, the lower third and the left side of the frame
darker and calm, quiet, precise, understated luxury craft, fine film grain. No
text, no letters, no calligraphy, no logos, no people, no hands.
