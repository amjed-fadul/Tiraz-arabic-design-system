Tiraz sample reference — Menu review / مراجعة القائمة
Fictional local content for component demonstration. No live retrieval.

Eight fictional usability sessions were scripted for this example.
Five of eight participants in the scripted scenario requested Arabic item names beside English names. The sample
menu brief requires bilingual names and clearly associated prices.
